Hard Case 11
==============================

Type:
Hard Spam

Message:
Your subscription is ready. Please review your account settings to avoid losing access to your service.

Correct Answer:
Spam

Reason:
Contains subscription, account, and access-related language. These patterns are common in phishing or promotional spam.



==============================
Hard Case 12
==============================

Type:
Hard Ham

Message:
I won the game yesterday! Everyone on the team was so happy.

Correct Answer:
Ham

Reason:
The word "won" frequently appears in spam messages, but here it describes a normal conversation about a game.



==============================
Hard Case 13
==============================

Type:
Hard Ham

Message:
Can you check the price of the new phone before we buy it?

Correct Answer:
Ham

Reason:
Although words such as "price" and "phone" may appear in commercial messages, the context is a normal personal conversation.
